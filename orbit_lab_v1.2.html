On: a new body orbits the center of mass of all heavier bodies inside its orbit, so a planet placed outside a binary circles both stars. Inside a planet's or star's sphere of influence it orbits that body alone (moons, or a planet hugging one star).

Pick a body, then tap the canvas. Drag from the tap point to throw it: the arrow sets its speed and direction, and a dashed line previews the path. 1.41× circular speed is escape speed.
