Tap a part to connect it to the selected shaft, already in mesh. Drag a part to drop it anywhere, or onto a hub to stack it on that shaft. Each part takes one layer on its shaft, and a shaft can hold up to 12 (layer 1 is the back). A bevel gear turns the drive 90°: tap it to start a sideways shaft from the selected one. Set sizes and add more parts in the Selected panel. Gears only mesh with gears on the same layer.
